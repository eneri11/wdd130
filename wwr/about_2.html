Our History

Founded in 2005 by a group of avid rafters, Exciting Expeditions began with a simple goal: to share the exhilaration of whitewater rafting with as many people as possible. From humble beginnings on the local river, we've grown into a premier adventure outfitter, known for our expert guides, commitment to safety, and passion for the outdoors. We've navigated countless rapids and created lifelong memories for thousands of adventurers. Our journey is one of growth, dedication, and a deep love for the rivers we call home.
