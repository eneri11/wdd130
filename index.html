Philippines

Marikina City is a highly urbanized city located on the eastern side of Metro Manila. It is widely known as the “Shoe Capital of the Philippines” because it produces about 70% of the countries shoes and is home to the famous Shoe Museum and many local footwear manufacturers. The city sits in the fertile Marikina Valley along the Marikina River and has grown from a small agricultural settlement founded by Jesuit missionaries in 1630 into a modern residential and commercial city. Today, it is known for its clean streets, organized urban planning, bike-friendly roads, and strong local craftsmanship.
